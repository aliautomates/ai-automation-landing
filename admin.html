Client finding workflow

Follow these steps for every batch. The score tells you where to spend your time first.
  1. Define your ideal clientSet industries, markets and a minimum budget in Settings. Scoring uses them.
  2. Find companies that fitUse public business listings, referrals, industry directories and your own research. Record the source of every lead.
  3. Record the basis for contactEvery lead needs a contact basis (inbound, referral, public listing, or opted in). Leads without one are not contacted.
  4. Import or add the leadAdd one lead below, or paste a CSV to import many. Duplicates by email or company are skipped.
  5. Reach out with a templateUse the templates below. Each includes a clear opt-out line. Log every message you send.
  6. Follow up on scheduleSet a follow-up date. Due follow-ups appear on the Overview. Mark do-not-contact at once if someone opts out.

How scoring works

Scores are out of 100 and explained on each lead.
+25 industry matches your ideal client list
+20 estimated value meets your minimum budget (or +5 for any value)
+15 warm source: referral or website enquiry
+10 market set, email on file, website on file, contact basis recorded
Reset to 0 if the lead carries the do-not-contact tag

Connect your Make.com scenarios

Each automation below is a Make.com scenario that starts with a Custom webhook. Paste the webhook URL here and switch the automation on. The panel sends a test payload and records every run.
  1. In Make.com, create a scenario whose first module is Webhooks → Custom webhook. Copy the URL it shows.
  2. Add the actions you need after the webhook, such as Gmail, Slack, Google Sheets or Supabase (use a server-side key inside Make, never in this page).
  3. Run it once with Redetermine data structure, then paste the URL below and click Send test.
  4. Turn the automation on when it works. Schedule-type automations should be set to run on Make's own schedule.
